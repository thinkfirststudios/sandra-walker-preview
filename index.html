The area

Westchester and Putnam Counties

From Long Island Sound to the Hudson Highlands, these two counties take in harbors, river towns, lakes and wooded hills. Here are some of the places I work.

Westchester County

New Rochelle

A Long Island Sound shoreline of harbors and islands, and a downtown station on the New Haven Line.

Scarsdale

A Tudor-style village center gathered around its Harlem Line station, with tall trees over winding streets.

Rye

On the Sound, with the Art Deco boardwalk of Playland and the shoreline paths of the Edith G. Read Sanctuary.

White Plains

The county seat, with a downtown of shops, restaurants and offices around one of the busiest stations on the Harlem Line.

Larchmont

A village on the Sound, with a waterfront park on the rocks of Larchmont Manor and a short main street up from the New Haven Line.

Bronxville

Tudor and stucco buildings around a village center on the Harlem Line, with the Bronx River Reservation along its edge.

Tarrytown

A Hudson River village where Main Street runs down toward the water, the Hudson Line and the Mario M. Cuomo Bridge.

Chappaqua

Wooded hills, old stone walls, and a stone station on the Harlem Line at the center of the hamlet.

Putnam County

Cold Spring

A Hudson River village where Main Street runs down to the water, with the Highlands rising all around.

Carmel

The county seat, on the shore of Lake Gleneida, with a historic courthouse near the water.

Brewster

A village gathered around its Harlem Line station, with brick buildings and a street clock at the crossroads.

Mahopac

A lakeside hamlet in the Town of Carmel, built around the shores of Lake Mahopac.

Patterson

Open valley land along the Great Swamp, with hills on either side and a station on the Harlem Line.

How it works

Buying and selling

Every move is different, but the way I work stays the same. We talk first, I listen, and you always know what comes next.

Buying a home or investment property

  1. 01

    We start with a conversation

    Tell me what you’re looking for, where you’d like to be and what matters most to you. If it’s your first time, we’ll take it one question at a time.

  2. 02

    We look at properties together

    Single-family homes, multi-family homes or investment properties across Westchester and Putnam. We’ll visit them together and talk each one through.

  3. 03

    I stay with you to the keys

    From your offer to the day you close, I’ll keep in touch at every stage so you’re never left wondering.

Selling a home or investment property

  1. 01

    We sit down together

    Whether it’s your home, a multi-family or an investment property, I want to hear your plans and what you’d like to happen next.

  2. 02

    We get it ready

    We’ll prepare the property to be seen and agree together on how it’s presented.

  3. 03

    I see it through with you

    When offers come in, we’ll talk through each one, and I’ll keep you informed all the way to closing.
