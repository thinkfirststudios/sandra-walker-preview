New Rochelle

A Long Island Sound shoreline of harbours and islands, and a downtown station on the New Haven Line.

Photo: Jas Cheng · CC BY-SA 4.0

Scarsdale

A Tudor-style village centre gathered around its Harlem Line station, with tall trees over winding streets.

Photo: ChangChienFu · Public domain

Rye

On the Sound, with the Art Deco boardwalk of Playland and the shoreline paths of the Edith G. Read Sanctuary.

Photo: Paul Sableman · CC BY 2.0

White Plains

The county seat — a downtown of shops, restaurants and offices around one of the busiest stations on the Harlem Line.

Photo: Thunderbombman · CC BY-SA 4.0

Larchmont

A village on the Sound, with a waterfront park on the rocks of Larchmont Manor and a short main street up from the New Haven Line.

Photo: Trxr4kds · Public domain

Bronxville

Tudor and stucco buildings around a village centre on the Harlem Line, with the Bronx River Reservation along its edge.

Photo: Jim Henderson · CC BY 4.0

Tarrytown

A Hudson River village where Main Street runs down toward the water, the Hudson Line and the Mario M. Cuomo Bridge.

Photo: Daniel Case · CC BY-SA 3.0

Chappaqua

Wooded hills, old stone walls, and a stone station on the Harlem Line at the centre of the hamlet.
